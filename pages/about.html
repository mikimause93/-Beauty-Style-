Who We Are

A Sanctuary of Beauty & Self-Expression

Founded in 2012 in the heart of Milan, Beauty & Style began as a small boutique salon with a big vision: to create a space where every client could discover their most confident, beautiful self. What started as a two-person team has grown into a full-service beauty destination with over 12 specialists across every discipline of beauty and wellness.

We believe that beauty is deeply personal. Our approach is never one-size-fits-all — every treatment, every recommendation, and every appointment is tailored to the unique individual sitting in our chair. We combine technical mastery with genuine care, and that philosophy has made us one of the most trusted beauty destinations in Northern Italy.
